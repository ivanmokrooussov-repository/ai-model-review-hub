This notice explains what AI Model & Tool Review Hub does with information. It is written for a simple static website. It is not legal advice.

**Last updated: November 25, 2026.**

## Who we are

The site is published by Ivan. This website does not publish an email address. Questions go to the [Gumroad profile](https://mokrooussov.gumroad.com).

## What this website collects

The pages are static files. There is no account, no login, and no comment box. There is no contact form. We do not run analytics or advertising scripts, and we do not set tracking cookies.

If you only read the site, we do not ask for your name, your email address, or any payment details.

## Hosting

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. When your browser requests a page, Cloudflare processes the technical data needed to deliver it, such as your IP address, browser type, and the page you requested. Cloudflare describes that processing in its own privacy policy: [https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

We do not use those logs to build a marketing profile, and we do not sell them.

## Messages

This site has no email address and no contact form. A note sent through the [Gumroad profile](https://mokrooussov.gumroad.com) is handled by Gumroad under Gumroad's privacy policy. We do not run a mailing list from this website.

## Payments

We do not take payment on this site. If you buy the Starter Pack, checkout happens on Gumroad. If you follow a vendor or affiliate link, checkout happens on that company's site. Those companies have their own privacy policies. We do not receive your card number.

## Cookies and saved preferences

This site does not set analytics cookies or advertising cookies.

If you use the theme button, your browser stores a light or dark preference on your device (`localStorage`). That value is not sent to us. If you never press the button, we do not store it. Your browser may still keep functional cookies that Cloudflare sets in order to serve the site.

## Links to other sites

Guides link to vendor documentation and may, later, link to affiliate offers. Those sites are outside this policy.

## Children

This site is written for adults making work decisions. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Sale of personal information

We do not sell personal information. We do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. There is no advertising network on the site.

## Changes

If we add analytics, a form, or any other collection later, we will update this page at the same time and change the date above. Affiliate programs often ask for a privacy policy before they approve a site. This page exists so the site can be reviewed for those programs without adding trackers first.
