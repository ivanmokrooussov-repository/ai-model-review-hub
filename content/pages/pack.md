The Starter Pack puts the workflows from this site in one download: prompts you can reuse, a spreadsheet for API bills, and a one-page way to pick a model tier. It is not another chat subscription.

## What you get

- **Prompt workflows for everyday work.** Short instructions for email drafts, rewriting in your own tone, checking a research note against sources, and cleaning messy notes before they go into a document or a sheet.
- **An LLM cost calculator spreadsheet.** The same arithmetic as the [API cost table](/api-pricing/) on this site: requests per month, average input tokens, average output tokens, and a monthly estimate from list prices. You can change the prices when a vendor does.
- **A one-page model checklist.** Three tiers — fast and cheap, everyday workhorse, and deep research — so you can decide what to open before you pay for a flagship plan. The reasoning is written up in [How to Pick an AI Model for Everyday Work](/guides/how-to-pick-an-ai-model-for-everyday-work/).

## Sample prompts

These three are previews. The download includes the full set.

**Email, from rough notes**

> Turn the notes below into a short email. Keep my facts exactly. Use plain sentences. Do not add praise, urgency, or a sign-off I did not ask for. Audience: [who]. Goal: [what should happen next]. Notes: [paste].

**Research check**

> List the factual claims in the draft below. For each claim, say whether I need a primary source before I publish it. Do not invent a citation. If you are not sure, say you are not sure. Draft: [paste].

**Cost check before a batch job**

> I will run [N] requests. Each request is about [input] input tokens and [output] output tokens. Using only the prices I paste below, compute the monthly cost. Show the arithmetic. Do not recommend a more expensive model unless I ask. Prices: [paste].
