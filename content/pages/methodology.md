This page is the testing and AI-content disclosure for AI Model & Tool Review Hub. Read it with the [affiliate disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/).

## Official sources come first

Plan names, free-tier limits, and API prices are taken from the provider's own pricing page, help center, or API documentation. Each guide lists those links and the date we checked them. The launch guides were checked on November 25, 2026.

If a number on this site disagrees with the vendor's current page, trust the vendor page. Then tell us on the [Gumroad profile](https://mokrooussov.gumroad.com), and we will correct the guide.

## AI-assisted editorial testing

We use AI tools while producing the site. That covers two jobs, and we keep them separate:

- **Drafting.** An assistant helps outline and draft a guide. A person checks the draft before it is published.
- **Try-outs.** For notes about tone or structure, we give several assistants the same ordinary tasks. The email guide, for example, uses a short client update, a polite invoice reminder, a vendor inquiry, and a reply that summarizes a six-message thread.

Try-outs are structured comparisons of real tasks. They are not laboratory benchmarks, and we do not turn them into scores.

## No fabricated scores

We do not invent accuracy percentages, win rates, or leaderboard ranks. When a guide says one assistant is a better fit for a job, that sentence is a practical judgment from the try-out and the published price, and the page says so.

API cost examples are arithmetic. The monthly figures use the published price per million tokens and the token counts written next to the example. You can recompute them on the [API cost table](/api-pricing/).

## What we do not claim

- A guide is a snapshot, not a live feed. Vendors change prices and caps without notice.
- A pleasant draft is not a fact-checked draft. Read customer-facing text before you send it.
- Nothing here is legal, financial, tax, or professional advice. The reminder about Canada's anti-spam rules (CASL) in the email guide is a prompt to check the law, not a legal opinion.
- We do not accept payment to invent a ranking. When a commercial relationship exists, the disclosure pages say so.

## Corrections

If you spot a stale price or a broken source link, write through the [Gumroad profile](https://mokrooussov.gumroad.com). Include the guide address and the official page you checked.
