## Affiliate links

Some pages may include affiliate links. If you click one and buy something, or start a paid plan, we may earn a commission from that company.

You do not pay extra because you used our link. The commission is paid by the company. It is not added to your bill.

A commission is a material connection between us and the company. We say so because that is the honest way to publish, and because Canadian advertising rules — including the Competition Act and Ad Standards guidance — expect a clear, upfront disclosure of that connection. This page is the full notice. Every guide also shows a short version at the top, which is where you will see it while you read.

As of the first version of this site, the guides link out to official pricing and help pages. Those source links are citations, not affiliate links. When a partner link is added, this notice still applies, and the line at the top of the guide stays in place.
