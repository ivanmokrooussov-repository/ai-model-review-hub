Writing email is one of the highest-return everyday uses for an AI assistant—not because you want robotic templates, but because turning three messy bullet points into a clear, polite message takes 20 seconds instead of 10 minutes.

The catch in late 2026 is that the major assistants differ sharply on **free limits**, **inbox integration**, and **default writing tone**. Below is how ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok compare for real-world email work.

## Qualitative Takeaways from Our AI-Assisted Email Tests

During our AI-assisted editorial evaluation across four common scenarios (short client status update, polite invoice reminder, concise vendor inquiry, and summarizing a 6-message thread into a reply), three practical patterns stood out:

### 1. Best Overall Writing Tone: Claude (Free & Pro)
- **Why it works:** Out of the box, Claude tends to avoid overly dramatic openers ("I hope this email finds you well in these fast-paced times!") when given a short role and audience instruction. Using Claude's **Projects** feature (up to 5 on the Free tier) lets you save a one-page "How I Write Emails" style guide once so every draft matches your tone.
- **Limitation:** The Free plan enforces rolling 5-hour and weekly usage caps, so heavy daily email bursts will hit the ceiling faster than ChatGPT Free.

### 2. Best Free Everyday Option: ChatGPT Free
- **Why it works:** OpenAI's Free tier provides unlimited everyday text chats (subject to abuse-prevention safeguards), making it the easiest zero-cost workhorse if you draft dozens of short messages a day and don't mind copying and pasting into your email client.
- **Limitation:** File uploads, image tools, and advanced reasoning have separate Free rate limits, and default drafts can sound slightly formulaic unless your prompt forbids corporate clichés.

### 3. Best Inside Gmail (Lowest Paid Price): Google Gemini + Google AI Plus
- **Why it works:** Many people assume Gemini inside Gmail is free, but Google's pricing chart lists **Gemini in Gmail, Docs, and Vids** starting on paid plans—specifically **Google AI Plus at $4.99/month** (with 400 GB storage) or **Google AI Pro at $19.99/month**. At $4.99/month, it is the cheapest native inbox option if you already use Gmail daily.
- **Limitation:** Free Gemini gives you the standalone web app (Flash-Lite model), not the inline Compose/Reply button inside Gmail.

### 4. Best Direct, Low-Fluff Outreach: Grok
- **Why it works:** When prompted for short, plain-English outreach or summarizing live company news before writing a note, Grok keeps drafts concise and avoids padded paragraphs. Paid entry starts at $8/month (Plus).
- **Limitation:** Weekly usage limits apply on the Free tier, and it does not sit natively inside Gmail or Outlook without external tools.

## A Simple Rule Before You Send AI-Drafted Emails

Never send an AI email draft unread. Always check three things before hitting Send:
1. **Facts and numbers:** Verify dates, prices, attachments, and recipient names.
2. **Tone match:** Strip out any exaggerated praise or generic "synergy" phrasing.
3. **Compliance:** If you send commercial outreach in Canada, follow CASL consent rules—never use AI to blast unsolicited bulk email lists.
