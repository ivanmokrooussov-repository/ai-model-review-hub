## 4 Questions to Ask Before Picking a Tool

### 1. Do You Need Fresh Web Facts or Just Rewriting?
- If you are **rewriting your own notes** (emails, SOPs, product descriptions), any Tier 1 or Tier 2 chat model works without live search.
- If you need **current 2026 prices, laws, or vendor docs**, use a search-grounded tool like **Perplexity**, **Grok**, **Claude Web Search**, or **Gemini Deep Research**, and click at least two primary links to verify numbers yourself.

### 2. Are You Working Interactively or Running Repeat Tasks?
- **Interactive (1–15 prompts/day):** Consumer web apps (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok) are simpler and include file uploads, canvas previews, and memory.
- **Repeatable or bulk tasks:** Moving to an API + spreadsheet or small script can cut costs by 80%–95%. For example, processing 1,000 short summaries on `gpt-5-nano` or `Claude Haiku 5.5` costs well under **$1 USD**.

### 3. How Long Is Your Context?
Watch pricing cliffs! In November 2026:
- Anthropic's **Claude Haiku 5.5** jumps 5x once a prompt exceeds **100,000 tokens** ($0.10/$0.50 -> $0.50/$2.50 per 1M tokens).
- xAI's **Grok 4.7** and **Grok 4.3** double their rate once a prompt hits **200,000 tokens**, billing the whole request at the long-context tier.
- Keeping prompts focused (and using prompt caching when repeating system instructions) keeps bills predictable.

### 4. What Is Your Human Review Step?
AI saves drafting time, not responsibility. For any customer-facing email, spreadsheet formula, or public article:
- Spot-check dates, dollar amounts, and links against official sources.
- Strip filler phrases and match your own voice.
- Add clear disclosures if you publish affiliate links or AI-assisted reviews.
